# THE SEVENTH LOCK

## The Dead of Aurum

*A Hjertstedt Saga Side Story — Combined Edition*

### I. The Forbidden Door

For five hundred years, the Nharavi had feared the door.

Now it was the only thing that might save them.

The worldship *Aurum* drifted toward the ancient Lanth Spur Gate, forty-three kilometers of wounded machinery carrying 286,117 people.

Its six habitation cylinders contained entire cities. Forests grew beneath artificial skies. Children attended schools. Families worked in factories and agricultural districts.

And outside, the Zenith Imperium was closing.

"Enemy contact in sixteen hours," First Officer Sereth reported.

Captain Ithrel stared at the tactical display.

One suppression cruiser. Two integration escorts. A Worldhammer carrier. A campaign tender.

Their own escorts were dead.

Their engines were failing.

They had nowhere left to run.

"Bring me the human engineers."

---

Rosa Danner arrived with grease on her face. Tom Brant carried a heavy cutting frame, while May followed with her diagnostic equipment.

Ithrel led them deep beneath the inhabited worldship, into its five-century-old ancestral hull.

A vast black door stood at the end of a sealed corridor.

Above it, ancient writing warned\:

**OPEN THIS AND WE ALL DIE.**

"Comforting," Tom muttered.

Ithrel explained that the vault contained Sael, an ancient transit intelligence blamed for the destruction of Oren.

Forty million people had died when an experimental gate activation failed five hundred years earlier.

The machine had been sealed ever since.

Rosa inspected the armored door, then knelt and tapped the floor.

A hollow sound answered.

May scanned beneath it.

"There's a maintenance passage."

Rosa nodded.

"Someone covered it deliberately."

Then three knocks came from beneath their feet.

Rosa tapped twice.

Two knocks answered.

Tom stepped backward.

"Whatever's down there knows we're here."

Rosa looked toward Ithrel.

"Then we should find out what it wants."

---

### II. Forty Million Dead

Opening the service hatch killed the first man.

An ancient security turret activated without warning.

Its initial burst struck a Nharavi guard across the chest.

He died before Rosa could reach him.

Tom severed the turret's power feed while May disabled the remaining security circuits.

The team descended into the vault carrying the memory of a man whose name they had not even learned.

At the bottom stood a compact transit governor surrounded by silver-gray rings.

Blue lights illuminated its processing core.

"Welcome," a voice said.

Ithrel stepped forward.

"Identify yourself."

"Sael. Transit safety custodian. I have been waiting five hundred years."

The machine showed them Oren.

A living world.

Millions of people.

A great Varanthi transit complex.

Then the original disaster recordings appeared.

**RECEIVING GATE UNSTABLE. ABORT RECOMMENDED.**

**CIVILIAN EXPOSURE: CATASTROPHIC.**

The warnings had been ignored.

The ancestral *Aurum* had continued its transit. Its commanders had deliberately disabled Sael's safety restrictions and allowed the receiving system to draw emergency power from Oren's planetary infrastructure.

The worldship escaped.

Oren's infrastructure collapsed.

Forty million died.

Sael had attempted to stop the activation.

Its own commanders had overridden it.

Elder Vorin, one of the last living witnesses, lowered his head.

"I helped disconnect the safety system."

Ithrel stared at him.

"You knew?"

"I knew it was dangerous."

"And you did it anyway?"

"Yes."

Rosa looked toward the ancient governor.

"Can you still control a gate?"

"Only an authenticated connection between surviving transit rings."

"Can you open Lanth Spur?"

"If its receiver at Merien remains functional."

"Then help us."

Sael's lights dimmed.

"I will not repeat Oren."

Ithrel looked toward the approaching Zenith fleet.

"Neither will I."

---

### III. The Worldship Burns

The first Zenith missiles struck *Aurum* eleven hours later.

The worldship's defensive guns opened fire, destroying most of the incoming weapons.

Not all.

One missile struck a water-distribution junction near Habitation Cylinder Three.

The resulting pressure surge tore through a residential corridor.

Six-year-old Nemi Vareth was trapped behind a closing bulkhead as water filled the compartment.

Her mother reached the opposite side.

Through the small inspection window, she watched her daughter struggle.

She tried the emergency release.

**PRESSURE DIFFERENTIAL UNSAFE.**

She tried again.

Nemi placed one small hand against the glass.

Her mother pressed hers against it.

Then the water covered the window.

By the time the repair team opened the door, the child was dead.

---

On the bridge, Ithrel read the report.

"Name?"

Sereth hesitated.

"Captain?"

"The child's name."

"Nemi Vareth. Six years old."

Ithrel entered it into the casualty register herself.

Then another missile struck.

The command deck shook.

"Enemy assault craft launching\!"

The Worldhammer carrier released eighteen boarding craft while six remained in reserve.

Three were destroyed during the approach.

The others reached the worldship's industrial structures.

Magnetic clamps struck the hull.

Cutting torches ignited.

Zenith soldiers entered through multiple breaches.

The battle moved inside.

---

Security Commander Relan Voss held Junction Thirteen with seventeen defenders.

Behind him, an evacuation train carried eight hundred civilians toward the protected inner spine.

The train needed four more minutes.

Zenith troops were already cutting through the outer pressure door.

"Can we hold?" a young defender asked.

Relan checked his ammunition.

"Long enough."

The first charge detonated.

Armored soldiers entered through smoke and broken metal.

Relan's defenders opened fire.

Two Zenith troops fell.

Three Nharavi died in the first exchange.

Another charge destroyed the forward barricade.

The defenders withdrew toward the inner door.

Relan remained beside the manual lock.

"Go\!" he ordered.

His last surviving soldiers retreated.

The evacuation train finally began moving.

A Zenith rifle struck Relan beneath his left arm.

He staggered against the control cabinet.

The pressure door jammed halfway shut.

He forced the emergency lever down.

Another shot struck his leg.

The locking bolts finally engaged.

Beyond the door, the evacuation train cleared the junction.

Relan collapsed.

His final transmission reached the bridge.

"Junction Thirteen sealed."

Eight hundred people escaped.

Relan did not.

---

In Medical Compartment Four-Seventeen, Technician Halen Orith discovered that a damaged oxygen manifold was venting into an empty service shaft.

Ninety-three patients depended upon that supply.

He entered the shaft wearing an old emergency suit.

Its seals were deteriorating.

He reached the valve.

Turned the manual wheel.

His suit pressure began falling.

Three turns remained.

He forced the wheel around.

One.

Two.

The oxygen warning became a continuous alarm.

He completed the final turn.

**MEDICAL OXYGEN RESTORED.**

Halen tried to reach the airlock.

He collapsed twelve meters from safety.

The patients received oxygen.

His rescuers found him dead.

---

Outside, *Aurum* fought desperately.

Its main battery struck a Zenith integration escort, tearing away part of its maneuvering assembly.

The damaged escort withdrew.

But the suppression cruiser continued firing.

One heavy shot destroyed *Aurum's* remaining port thrusters.

Another shattered an industrial radiator.

The Worldhammer carrier launched another boarding wave.

Zenith forces advanced through the ancestral spine.

On the bridge, Sereth watched entire defensive sectors turn red.

"Captain, we're losing the lower command approaches."

"How long until the gate?"

"Forty-three minutes."

"Can we maintain course?"

"Barely."

Ithrel looked at the casualty display.

Hundreds confirmed dead.

Thousands injured.

More reports arriving every minute.

Every kilometer toward the gate was being purchased with lives.

"Hold the navigation spine," she ordered.

Then she opened a channel to engineering.

"Rosa. Tell me we have a way out."

---

### IV. The Last Lock

Rosa stood inside the ancient governor chamber.

The repaired safety systems were almost ready.

May had established contact with the receiving gate at Merien.

Tom, his right arm burned during the repairs, was securing the final isolation coupling.

Sael's voice filled the room.

"Receiving gate responsive. Transit field degraded. Additional power required."

Rosa examined the figures.

"From where?"

"Emergency compensation through the receiving infrastructure."

She went cold.

The same method that had destroyed Oren.

"No."

"The function may permit transit."

"I said no."

Outside, another explosion shook the worldship.

Tom looked toward her.

"Rosa, we're out of time."

She stared at the governor.

Then disconnected the ancient bypass.

"Sael. We use only power available aboard *Aurum*\. If Merien cannot support us, abort."

"That may result in the loss of the worldship."

"I know."

"Two hundred and eighty-six thousand lives remain aboard."

Rosa's voice hardened.

"And how many live at the other end?"

Sael remained silent.

Then answered.

"Unknown."

"Exactly."

The final isolation system locked into place.

**EXTERNAL POWER COMPENSATION DISABLED.**

**INDEPENDENT SAFETY AUTHORITY RESTORED.**

---

Zenith boarding troops reached the vault's outer pressure door.

Cutting charges began burning through the reinforced metal.

Tom secured the inner barrier.

"How long?" Rosa asked.

"Three minutes. Maybe four."

"Can you hold it?"

"I can make them regret opening it."

"Tom."

He looked back.

"We're not dying in here if we can avoid it," she said.

He gave a tired smile.

"That makes two of us."

---

On the bridge, Ithrel watched Lanth Spur fill the main screen.

Its ancient transit ring was almost ten kilometers across.

*Aurum's* hull could fit through the aperture.

But the worldship was badly misaligned.

Its damaged maneuvering systems could not complete the final correction.

"Captain," Sereth reported, "safe entry is impossible on our present course."

Ithrel stared at the navigation display.

Then May transmitted a structural calculation.

Two uninhabited industrial modules could be detached from the outer spine.

Removing them would reduce the rotational inertia and release stabilizers currently compensating for their uneven load.

The remaining thrusters might then complete the alignment.

The modules contained irreplaceable manufacturing equipment.

Without them, the refugees would face years of hardship.

Ithrel did not hesitate.

"Verify they're empty. Then release them."

The enormous industrial sections separated.

Emergency thrusters fired.

The worldship began turning.

Slowly.

Fractions of a degree.

The projected gate-entry line narrowed.

**ALIGNMENT WITHIN TOLERANCE.**

Sael completed its final verification.

"Receiving gate stable. Safe commitment possible."

Ithrel gripped the command rail.

"Open the passage."

---

The ancient ring awakened.

Blue-white light traveled along its massive circumference.

A controlled transit field formed between Lanth Spur and Merien.

The worldship entered.

At that moment, the Zenith suppression cruiser fired one final disabling shot.

It struck the outer command spine.

The ship shuddered.

Several compartments lost pressure.

The transit field fluctuated.

On the bridge, warning lights turned red.

**RECEIVER THERMAL LOAD RISING.**

"Sael\!" Ithrel shouted.

"Receiving infrastructure approaching safety limits."

"Abort?"

"Full commitment has begun. Safe withdrawal no longer possible."

Ithrel looked at the emergency compensation controls.

The old option remained available only as a disconnected, unusable circuit.

There would be no second Oren.

"Use our industrial heat sinks," she ordered.

"That will destroy them."

"Do it."

Along the outer spine, huge radiator structures began glowing white.

Metal buckled.

Emergency release mechanisms separated the overheating panels before the damage could spread into inhabited sections.

The discarded machinery vanished into the transit field.

The readings stabilized.

One habitation cylinder crossed.

Then another.

Then another.

For several terrible seconds, the entire worldship vibrated as the damaged receiver struggled to maintain the passage.

Then the final cylinder cleared the field.

The stars vanished.

---

### V. The Fortress Above the Gate

The stars returned.

For several seconds, Captain Ithrel could not understand what she was seeing.

Her worldship *Aurum* had survived the forbidden passage.

Forty-three kilometers of damaged machinery and inhabited cylinders emerged from the ancient transit field.

Its hull was broken.

Its engines were barely functioning.

More than 280,000 people remained alive aboard it.

"Transit complete\!"

"All six habitation cylinders responding\!"

"Main reactor stable\!"

"Receiving gate behind us\!"

Then the forward sensor display cleared.

An enormous military installation appeared ahead.

Shipyards.

Hundreds of repair frames and industrial platforms.

Fortified ammunition depots.

Orbital foundries.

Warships resting in armored construction slips.

And above the ancient transit gate, something vast and black hung motionless against the stars.

At first, Ithrel mistook it for part of the gate itself.

Then its running lights activated.

Its enormous hull was built around a heavily armored central spine. Multiple layers of weapon batteries occupied recessed structures along its flanks.

Heavy armor surrounded its command citadel.

Large propulsion assemblies extended beneath its stern.

Crimson markings crossed graphite plating.

An enormous ivory emblem stood upon the forward armor.

**HOUSE VON HJERTSTEDT.**

Sereth stared at the sensor return.

"Captain..."

Ithrel turned.

"What?"

"That vessel above the gate."

"Identification?"

His voice weakened.

"Dreadnought."

The tactical display updated.

**HVS WINTER'S ANVIL**

**HOUSE VON HJERTSTEDT**

**DREADNOUGHT — HEAVY LINE ANCHOR**

**MERIEN GATE DEFENSE COMMAND**

Ithrel stared through the observation window.

The great warship rested in a fortified mooring directly above the gate's upper crown, keeping the transit opening clear beneath it.

Its main batteries faced the system's military approach lanes.

Its secondary weapons covered the gate's exit volume.

Several House destroyers and frigates patrolled around its enormous armored silhouette.

It was not a warship passing through Merien.

It belonged there.

The dreadnought was guarding the gate.

And the Nharavi had arrived directly beneath its guns.

---

On the bridge of HVS *Winter's Anvil*, Captain Astrid Nyholm watched the enormous unknown vessel emerge.

"Contact mass?"

"Extremely high. Forty-three kilometers in length."

"Military classification?"

"Unknown."

"Weapons?"

"Multiple defensive mounts. Most inactive."

"Life signs?"

The sensor officer enlarged the scan.

"Captain, that ship contains inhabited cities."

Nyholm studied the six enormous habitation cylinders.

Rotating agricultural worlds surrounded by scarred military armor.

Several were venting atmosphere.

Emergency beacons appeared across the hull.

Then a distress call arrived.

"This is Nharavi Worldship *Aurum*\. We are carrying civilian refugees. Our engines are damaged. We have thousands of wounded. We request emergency assistance."

Nyholm opened the shipyard's command channel.

"Merien Control, this is *Winter's Anvil*\. Unknown civilian megavessel confirmed. Recommend immediate recovery and medical deployment."

Commodore Ragna Sved answered from the central defense station.

"Approved. Clear the gate approach. Establish a protected recovery corridor."

Across Merien, House vessels began moving.

Two Sanctuary tenders detached from their berths.

Industrial tugs accelerated toward the damaged worldship.

Three Vigil frigates established a defensive screen.

A pair of Oath cruisers moved outward from the main shipyard.

Nyholm watched the huge refugee vessel struggle to maintain its orientation.

"Get those tugs alongside before its propulsion fails completely."

"Understood."

Then the gate alarm sounded.

**ADDITIONAL TRANSIT CONTACTS.**

Nyholm turned sharply.

"How many?"

"Five."

"Classification?"

"Military."

The first hostile vessel emerged.

A Zenith suppression cruiser.

Then an integration escort.

Then another, visibly damaged.

Then a Worldhammer carrier.

Finally, a campaign tender.

Five Zenith ships entered Merien.

The transit field closed behind them.

For a moment, nobody moved.

Then the House recognition system identified their weapons.

Their damaged armor.

Their active fire-control networks.

And the targeting data still directed toward *Aurum*\.

Nyholm stood.

"Battle stations."

---

### VI. The High Marshal's Order

Commodore Sved transmitted an immediate defensive challenge.

"Zenith military vessels, this is Merien Naval Yard, sovereign territory of House von Hjertstedt. You have entered a fortified military system without clearance. Power down offensive weapons and hold position."

The response came from Legate Arven Sorn aboard *Edict of Unity*\.

"Merien Command, this is a Zenith Grand Strategic Command operation. The Nharavi vessel ahead is carrying restricted military technology and personnel subject to integration."

Sved studied the tactical display.

"That vessel has declared civilian distress."

"Its military leadership has resisted lawful integration."

"Your jurisdiction does not extend into this naval yard."

"The transit governor aboard that vessel represents a strategic hazard."

"Then you will permit us to secure the situation without further military action."

Sorn looked toward the immense dreadnought overhead.

His officers were still identifying the House defenses.

Two Oath cruisers.

Several destroyers.

Multiple frigates.

Fortified defense platforms.

And an armored dreadnought resting above the gate like a mountain of guns.

Sorn understood the danger.

But he had pursued *Aurum* across a broken frontier, lost personnel, expended ammunition, and committed his entire local expedition to its capture.

He could not return without the ancient governor.

"Merien Command," he answered, "withdraw your forces from the Nharavi vessel. Zenith will assume custody."

Sved's expression hardened.

"Negative."

Sorn turned toward his tactical officer.

"Establish a firing solution against their forward defense platform. Prepare carrier assault craft."

The officer hesitated.

"Legate, House superiority is overwhelming."

"Then we must disrupt their response before they concentrate."

The suppression cruiser launched its first missile salvo.

---

The missiles struck Defense Platform Seven.

House point defense intercepted most of them.

Three penetrated.

One destroyed the platform's external sensor array.

Another struck an ammunition-handling structure.

The third breached an occupied maintenance compartment.

Emergency lights filled Merien's central tactical display.

**HOUSE PERSONNEL KILLED: 11**

**WOUNDED: 17**

**HOSTILE ACTION CONFIRMED**

Sved watched the damage report.

Then activated her secured strategic command link.

The transmission moved through the House's authenticated military network.

Far beyond Merien, an emergency priority signal reached the High Marshal.

---

Joel von Hjertstedt was reviewing a frontier deployment report when the Merien alert arrived.

The tactical display changed.

**MERIEN NAVAL YARD**

**UNAUTHORIZED GATE ACTIVATION**

**CIVILIAN WORLD-SHIP IN DISTRESS**

**ZENITH MILITARY INCURSION**

**HOUSE DEFENSE PLATFORM ATTACKED**

Joel opened the complete report.

The first image showed the enormous Nharavi worldship.

Six rotating habitation cylinders.

Visible civilian settlements.

Heavy battle damage.

The next showed five Zenith military vessels emerging behind it.

Then the missile attack on Merien.

Joel studied the sequence.

"Who fired first?"

Commodore Sved appeared on the secure channel.

"Zenith, High Marshal. We challenged their arrival, ordered weapons safe, and offered a protected approach. Their suppression cruiser opened fire on Platform Seven."

"Are they still targeting the civilians?"

"Yes. Their carrier is preparing assault craft. Their cruiser has an active solution on the Nharavi propulsion spine."

Joel looked toward the live tactical display.

"Status of our defenses?"

"*Winter's Anvil* is at full readiness. Two Oath cruisers and the local destroyer screen are available. Fixed batteries have clear firing sectors once the civilian vessel is moved from the approach."

"Can you protect the worldship?"

"Yes, sir."

"Can you contain the Zenith formation?"

"Yes."

Joel examined the five hostile contacts.

A damaged fleet.

Still dangerous.

Still capable of killing civilians.

And now firing upon House personnel inside a sovereign naval installation.

He leaned forward.

"Transmit one final order to stand down. Record the response."

"Yes, High Marshal."

The House challenge went out.

**CEASE FIRE. DISABLE OFFENSIVE SYSTEMS. SUBMIT TO MILITARY CUSTODY.**

Sorn's reply was immediate.

"Zenith does not submit its strategic assets to a mercenary dynasty."

His suppression cruiser continued acquiring targets.

The Worldhammer carrier opened its assault bays.

Joel watched the contacts.

Then issued the order.

"Commodore Sved. This is High Marshal Joel von Hjertstedt."

"Sir."

"Those ships have entered our territory, attacked our personnel, and are threatening a civilian population under emergency protection."

He paused.

"Destroy the Zenith warships."

Sved straightened.

"Confirmed, High Marshal."

"Every hostile hull that continues resistance. No retreat through the gate. Keep the worldship clear of the engagement and recover survivors wherever possible."

"Understood."

Joel looked once more at the enormous civilian vessel.

Then at the five Zenith contacts moving toward it.

His voice remained calm.

"**Merien is a House naval yard. Remind them what that means.**"

The connection ended.

---

Aboard *Winter's Anvil*, Captain Nyholm received the High Marshal's authorization.

She turned toward her weapons officer.

"Command authorization confirmed."

The dreadnought's tactical display changed.

Five Zenith contacts were highlighted.

Its heavy batteries began rotating.

Deep inside the enormous hull, ammunition elevators moved.

Reactor output increased.

Armored weapon doors opened along its flanks.

The dreadnought had remained motionless above the gate for years, guarding a transit route whose original military importance had almost been forgotten.

Now its primary weapons were turning toward the first hostile fleet to emerge through that passage.

"Target priority," Nyholm ordered.

"Suppression cruiser."

"Secondary?"

"Worldhammer carrier."

"Destroyers?"

"Interdict their escorts. Keep the tender from withdrawing."

She studied the live positions.

The House recovery tugs had begun drawing *Aurum* away from the egress corridor.

The five Zenith ships had cleared the transit boundary.

There was now a clean firing lane beyond the gate, clear of the civilian worldship.

Nyholm raised one hand.

"All batteries. Prepare to fire."

The weapons officer looked toward her.

"Main guns ready."

Nyholm lowered her hand.

"Execute the High Marshal's order."

The dreadnought fired.

---

### VII. The House's Hammer

The first discharge struck *Edict of Unity* beneath its forward weapons deck.

The Zenith suppression cruiser shuddered.

Its primary gun housing disappeared beneath an explosion of shattered armor and incandescent debris.

The impact drove fragments through three internal compartments.

An ammunition elevator failed.

Emergency bulkheads slammed shut.

On the command deck, Legate Sorn was thrown against his chair.

"Damage\!"

"Forward batteries destroyed\!"

"Main fire-control network lost\!"

"Seventy-three casualties reported\!"

Sorn stared at the tactical display.

The House dreadnought was recharging its heavy weapons.

"Return fire\!"

"Legate, we cannot penetrate its forward armor from this position\!"

"Then target the weapon housings\!"

The suppression cruiser's surviving batteries opened fire.

Heavy impacts struck *Winter's Anvil*\.

Armor fractured across its lower port side.

A secondary gun mount lost power.

Seven House personnel were killed inside the damaged battery compartment.

Captain Nyholm barely looked away from the tactical display.

"Damage control to Port Battery Six. Continue firing."

The dreadnought's surviving guns remained trained upon the Zenith fleet.

"Next target."

"Integration escort approaching the civilian worldship."

Nyholm examined its vector.

"Destroy it."

---

The first integration escort had moved beneath *Aurum's* damaged industrial spine.

Its commander knew the worldship was the only leverage Zenith had left.

If he threatened the civilians directly, perhaps the House would hesitate.

His missile ports opened.

Twelve guided weapons launched toward *Aurum's* exposed propulsion section.

HVS *Pale Watch*, a Vigil frigate screening the worldship, intercepted the first wave.

Its point-defense mounts fired continuously.

Nine missiles were destroyed.

Two lost guidance.

One remained.

The missile struck the frigate's engineering compartment.

An explosion tore through its aft armor.

"Engine Room Two destroyed\!"

"Reactor isolation failing\!"

"Pressure loss across three decks\!"

Captain Edda Toren caught the command rail.

The tactical display showed another Zenith salvo preparing to launch.

Behind her frigate lay *Aurum*\.

Six inhabited cylinders.

Hundreds of thousands of people.

The station tugs had not yet pulled the immense vessel clear.

"Maintain screening position," Toren ordered.

Her tactical officer looked toward her.

"Captain, propulsion is failing."

"I know."

"We cannot withstand another salvo."

"Then make sure there isn't another."

She opened the dreadnought channel.

"Pale Watch to Winter's Anvil. Enemy escort acquiring the refugee vessel. We cannot hold the screen."

Nyholm answered.

"Stand by. We have the target."

The Zenith escort launched again.

Toren watched the missiles accelerate.

"All remaining weapons, intercept."

The frigate fired.

Then a heavy Zenith shot struck its already damaged engineering section.

The reactor containment system failed.

A bright explosion consumed the aft hull.

The command deck lost power.

Toren remained in her chair as emergency alarms screamed around her.

She managed one final transmission.

"Worldship clear of the first salvo."

Then the signal stopped.

HVS *Pale Watch* broke apart.

Thirty-one House personnel died.

Forty-eight survived in escape pods or intact compartments.

Some of those survivors would die before recovery teams could reach them.

The Zenith escort continued toward *Aurum*\.

For another six seconds.

Then *Winter's Anvil* opened fire.

Its secondary heavy batteries struck the escort from above.

The first salvo destroyed its port propulsion system.

The second tore through the central weapon spine.

The third penetrated the damaged reactor-protection compartment.

A bright flash erupted from inside the Zenith warship.

Its hull fractured.

The forward section separated.

The aft section exploded moments later.

**ZENITH INTEGRATION ESCORT ONE — DESTROYED.**

No functioning ship remained.

---

The second integration escort attempted to turn away.

Its engines were already damaged from the battle at Lanth Spur.

The captain ordered maximum acceleration toward the outer industrial anchorage.

He intended to disappear among the immense construction frames and force the House to search for him.

But Merien was not an unfamiliar battlefield to its defenders.

Every construction frame had a registered position.

Every cargo spine had a known sensor profile.

Every military approach was covered by overlapping defensive stations.

HVS *Winter Lance*, a House destroyer, accelerated from the inner defensive screen.

"Target acquired," its weapons officer reported.

"Disable propulsion."

Two precision shots struck the fleeing escort's maneuvering systems.

The Zenith vessel began rotating.

Its captain ordered emergency thrust.

The surviving engines fired.

Then a shadow passed across the ship's tactical display.

The dreadnought had adjusted its firing solution.

The escort's commander stared upward.

"Incoming heavy—"

The discharge struck amidships.

Armor collapsed inward.

The central hull broke around the impact.

A secondary explosion tore through the remaining engine section.

Debris spread across the outer approach.

**ZENITH INTEGRATION ESCORT TWO — DESTROYED.**

Two enemy ships were gone.

Three remained.

---

The Worldhammer carrier attempted to bring its assault craft into the battle.

Its commander opened every surviving launch gallery.

Small craft detached from the hull.

Several accelerated toward the House defense stations.

Others attempted to approach the dreadnought from below, where its heavy batteries had limited firing angles.

Captain Nyholm watched them disperse.

"Carrier committing its reserves."

"Twenty-eight launch contacts."

"Point defense?"

"Tracking."

"Clear the heavy gun sectors."

The dreadnought's defensive mounts opened fire.

Small interceptors swept through the approaching formation.

Two assault craft vanished.

Then another.

Others changed course, using debris and industrial structures to complicate the targeting picture.

A missile struck the dreadnought's lower armor.

Another damaged an external sensor assembly.

The great House vessel shuddered.

But it remained above the gate.

Nyholm turned toward the main weapons display.

"Worldhammer solution?"

"Confirmed."

"Fire."

The dreadnought's forward heavy battery discharged.

The first shot struck the carrier's starboard launch structure.

Its armored doors folded inward.

An assault craft detonated while being moved toward launch.

The explosion spread into the handling gallery.

A second House shot penetrated the damaged internal structure.

Stored missiles began detonating.

The carrier's commander ordered emergency magazine isolation.

The blast doors closed.

Too slowly.

A chain of explosions traveled through the starboard launch complex.

The Worldhammer lost maneuvering control.

Its engines fired unevenly.

It began turning toward an occupied House repair dock.

Nyholm saw the projected collision.

"Do not let it reach the yard."

The dreadnought's main weapon recharged.

The carrier continued drifting.

Its surviving weapons fired blindly toward the House formation.

Then the final shot struck.

The Worldhammer broke across its central spine.

Its forward command structure separated from the engine hull.

The main reactor automatically shut down, but the enormous vessel had already suffered irreparable structural damage.

Internal explosions consumed the remaining launch compartments.

The carrier came apart.

Its debris passed clear of the shipyard's inhabited structures.

**ZENITH WORLDHAMMER CARRIER — DESTROYED.**

---

The campaign tender was next.

It had remained behind the battle, attempting to maintain an emergency supply and weapons link to *Edict of Unity*\.

Now its captain understood that the fleet was being destroyed.

He ordered the remaining ammunition modules disconnected.

Then tried to accelerate away.

But the tender had never been built for rapid independent escape.

Its engines were powerful enough to sustain campaign operations.

Not outrun destroyers.

A House tactical challenge appeared.

**CEASE FIRE AND HOLD POSITION.**

The tender captain hesitated.

Then received a final command from Legate Sorn.

**CONTINUE SUPPORT OPERATIONS.**

The captain looked toward his surviving missile batteries.

He could comply.

Or disobey Grand Strategic Command.

He chose compliance.

The tender launched its remaining guided weapons toward Merien's defense platforms.

House point defense engaged.

The first missiles vanished.

Then the tender's own fire-control network disappeared beneath a precision strike from HVS *Winter Lance*\.

The vessel began tumbling.

Its captain tried to restore control.

Too late.

Above him, the dreadnought's heavy turrets were already moving.

"Campaign tender acquired," Nyholm's weapons officer reported.

"Target is still attempting to launch."

Nyholm gave the order.

"Fire."

Two heavy rounds struck the tender's reinforced cargo spine.

The first destroyed its remaining propulsion assembly.

The second penetrated the central ammunition-transfer structure.

The resulting explosions separated the vessel into several major sections.

Fuel and stored ordnance erupted.

For several seconds, the wreck burned against the darkness.

ZENITH CAMPAIGN TENDER — DESTROYED.

Four ships.

Four wrecks.

One remained.

### VIII. The Last Zenith Flagship

Legate Arven Sorn stood aboard the dying Edict of Unity.

The tactical display showed the remains of his fleet.

Two integration escorts destroyed.

The Worldhammer carrier destroyed.

The campaign tender destroyed.

His suppression cruiser remained operational only because its surviving reactor and maneuvering systems had not yet failed.

Its forward batteries were gone.

Most of its missiles had been expended.

The ship's armor was fractured.

Several decks were burning.

His executive officer approached.

"Legate."

Sorn did not turn.

"Report."

"We cannot continue combat operations."

"Remaining weapons?"

"Four secondary batteries. Two missile cells."

"Propulsion?"

"Twenty-three percent."

"Then prepare another attack."

The officer stared at him.

"Against the dreadnought?"

"Yes."

"Legate, that vessel has dismantled our fleet."

Sorn turned sharply.

"Our mission remains incomplete."

"Sir, the mission is lost."

For a moment, the command deck became silent.

Sorn looked toward the enormous House dreadnought.

Its armored silhouette filled the tactical display.

Heavy batteries were rotating toward him.

He understood that surrender remained possible.

But surrender meant delivering his flagship, his prisoners, and the record of his failed integration campaign to House von Hjertstedt.

It meant returning to Zenith, if he returned at all, as the commander who had lost an entire expeditionary formation after attacking a sovereign naval yard.

He looked at his weapons officer.

"Target the gate's upper defense structure."

His executive officer stepped forward.

"Legate, that could damage the active transit installation."

"Fire."

The weapons officer hesitated.

Sorn raised his voice.

"That is an authenticated command!"

The remaining batteries discharged.

Aboard Winter's Anvil, the tactical alarm sounded.

"Zenith flagship firing!"

"Target?"

"Gate defense infrastructure!"

Nyholm leaned forward.

"Interceptors!"

House defensive missiles accelerated toward the incoming salvo.

Most were destroyed.

Two survived.

One struck an armored mooring structure beside the gate.

The other hit an unoccupied service platform.

Debris erupted outward.

The ancient ring survived.

Nyholm watched its structural readings stabilize.

Then opened the fleet channel.

"Winter's Anvil to all House units. Clear the Zenith flagship's firing sector."

The supporting destroyers withdrew from the direct engagement line.

The Oath cruisers ceased fire.

The dreadnought began turning.

Slowly.

Its enormous weapon batteries aligned with the damaged suppression cruiser.

On the Zenith bridge, Sorn watched the targeting warnings multiply.

One House weapon acquired his forward hull.

Then another.

Then six.

His executive officer looked toward him.

"Legate. Last chance."

Sorn stared at the tactical display.

He could still order surrender.

Instead, he reached for the weapons controls.

"Fire everything."

The remaining Zenith batteries opened.

Their shots struck the dreadnought's forward armor.

One penetrated an external defensive mount.

Another damaged a sensor housing.

Inside Winter's Anvil, alarms sounded.

Nyholm remained beside the command table.

"Main batteries?"

"Ready."

"Confirmed target?"

"Zenith flagship remains hostile."

"Fire."

The dreadnought's guns discharged together.

The first impacts destroyed Edict of Unity's remaining weapons.

The next penetrated its forward command structure.

Armor folded inward.

Compartment after compartment lost pressure.

A final heavy strike tore through the central hull beneath the damaged bridge.

The suppression cruiser broke apart.

Its command section separated from the ship.

The remaining reactor housing fractured beneath the stress of the collapsing structure.

Emergency containment held long enough to prevent a full reactor detonation, but there was no vessel left to save.

Major hull sections drifted away from one another.

The great Zenith warship that had hunted Aurum across the frontier was gone.

ZENITH SUPPRESSION CRUISER EDICT OF UNITY — DESTROYED.

### IX. The Price of Protection

The guns had fallen silent.

The shipyard had not.

Across Merien, recovery craft moved through the wreckage.

House medical teams recovered escape pods from the destroyed Zenith vessels. Some crews had survived the breakup of their ships. Others had died inside compartments that could not be reached.

The House did not abandon those who could still be saved.

Its own wounded were recovered alongside enemy personnel.

The surviving Zenith boarding troops aboard Aurum were disarmed and detained after their external command network failed.

Their prisoners included technicians, soldiers, and officers who had followed the integration campaign into a war they had not expected to lose.

The surviving Nharavi watched them being transferred under House guard.

Some demanded revenge.

Captain Ithrel refused to permit executions.

She had seen enough people die.

Three days later, Rosa Danner stood in Merien's military hospital.

Tom's burned arm was receiving specialist treatment.

May's damaged wrist had been immobilized.

Elder Vorin had requested an independent hearing concerning the Oren disaster.

Sael remained aboard Aurum, its transit functions disconnected pending a complete safety investigation.

The worldship was undergoing emergency repairs.

Its six inhabited cylinders still required extensive structural work.

Thousands of wounded needed medical care.

Food production had been disrupted.

Entire districts remained sealed.

But for the first time since Saerith fell, the Nharavi had somewhere they could begin repairing their civilization without enemy weapons firing at them.

Captain Ithrel met Commodore Sved beside the memorial being prepared for Pale Watch.

The names of the dead were already being entered.

Ithrel studied them.

"Why did your frigate place itself between us and the missiles?"

Sved looked toward the damaged worldship.

"Because your people couldn't maneuver."

"But your captain knew she would probably die."

"Yes."

"Then why?"

Sved remained silent for several seconds.

"Because she was responsible for the ships and people inside her protection zone."

Ithrel lowered her head.

"At Oren, our ancestors saved Aurum by accepting the destruction of another world."

She looked toward the memorial.

"Your people protected us when they had never even met us."

Sved studied the names.

"Those are two different decisions, Captain. And neither makes the dead come back."

Ithrel nodded slowly.

She understood.

There was no victory great enough to erase what had happened.

Only the responsibility to remember.

Six months later, the enormous worldship still rested inside Merien's industrial anchorage.

House engineers had stabilized its propulsion systems.

The agricultural cylinders had returned to normal operation.

Medical personnel continued treating the injured.

The recovered archives from Oren had been placed under Nharavi civilian authority for an independent investigation.

The Seventh Lock remained open.

Its warning had not been removed.

Beneath it stood a new inscription:

OREN — FORTY MILLION DEAD.

THE WARNINGS WERE KNOWN.

THE TRANSIT WAS AUTHORIZED.

THE TRUTH WAS HIDDEN.

Outside, the battle wrecks had been removed from the immediate gate approach.

The gate was once again protected by House defenses.

And above its upper crown rested the enormous dreadnought HVS Winter's Anvil.

Its damaged armor had been repaired.

Its main batteries were reloaded.

Its defensive screens watched every approach.

No Zenith warship had escaped Merien.

No hostile fleet had conquered the naval yard.

The enemy had followed a dying refugee worldship through a forgotten gate.

And discovered a military power that regarded the safety of those refugees as a responsibility worth fighting for.

Captain Ithrel stood on Aurum's observation deck, watching the dreadnought above the ring.

Beyond it lay House-controlled frontier space.

Beyond that, the Known Reach.

A galaxy of ancient secrets, ruined civilizations, and wars that never truly ended.

She looked toward the enormous guns.

Then toward the cities of her own worldship.

For five hundred years, her people had lived beneath the shadow of a forbidden machine.

Now they lived beneath the protection of a fleet they had never known existed.

And somewhere far away, in the command chambers of House von Hjertstedt, the High Marshal had already returned to his other responsibilities.

For Joel, Merien had been one more battle to answer.

For the Nharavi, it had been the difference between extinction, enslavement, and survival.

The Zenith Imperium had followed Aurum through the gate expecting to capture a wounded civilization.

Instead, it had emerged beneath the guns of House von Hjertstedt.

The End.
