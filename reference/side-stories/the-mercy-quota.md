# The Mercy Quota — historical lore and story reference

The owner directed on 11 November 2026 that this story be added as historical lore. Its narrative takes place during Earth's early United Federation Reconstruction, before the interstellar lane era. It is separate from the current main novel, and does not establish an exact date or a relationship to individual modern characters. [Story](../../side-stories/the-mercy-quota.md) · [Source record](../../sources/mercy-quota-2026-11-11.json).

## Full-story spoilers

- Mara Vale recalls the forced relocation of her family from Shelter Stack Forty-Six to Greyfen, and the population-selection program under WARDEN and the Reconstruction Ministry.
- Tomas Vale, Mara's father, signs a power-allocation order benefiting settlements while people remain dependent on the shelter systems. Mara keeps records of people who were lost or whose fates remain uncertain.
- The account portrays Reconstruction as both a recovery and a coercive, deadly process. Official records and Mara's memories do not provide a complete count of losses.

## Continuity and editorial notes

- The narrator shares the name Mara Vale with the much later [captain of the Resolute Dawn](../characters/mara-vale.md). Their identity or family relationship is not established; the historical setting and the later captain's setting do not by themselves establish that they are the same person.
- Mara calls Elin her granddaughter and identifies Tomas as her father, but refers to Tomas as Elin's “great-great-grandfather”. The original wording is preserved pending an editorial decision.
- The source draft contains section headings and exceeds the current 6,000-word guideline for new standalone side stories. It is retained as requested for historical-lore and narration review without an unapproved prose cut.
- The source conversation's original postscript labeled the lore proposed. The owner's later direction to add it as historical lore supersedes that status label; the preserved source still records it for provenance.
