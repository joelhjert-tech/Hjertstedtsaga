# The Mercy Quota — historical lore and story reference

The owner directed on 11 November 2026 that this story be added as historical lore. Its narrative takes place during Earth's early United Federation Reconstruction, before the interstellar lane era. It is separate from the current main novel, and does not establish an exact date or a relationship to individual modern characters. [Story](../../side-stories/the-mercy-quota.md) · [Source record](../../sources/mercy-quota-2026-11-11.json).

## Full-story spoilers

- Mira Dalen recalls the forced relocation of her family from Shelter Stack Forty-Six to Greyfen, and the population-selection program under WARDEN and the Reconstruction Ministry.
- Tomas Dalen, Mira's father, signs a power-allocation order benefiting settlements while people remain dependent on the shelter systems. Mira keeps records of people who were lost or whose fates remain uncertain.
- The account portrays Reconstruction as both a recovery and a coercive, deadly process. Official records and Mira's memories do not provide a complete count of losses.

## Continuity and editorial notes

- The original draft named the historical protagonist Mara Vale, the same name as the later [captain of the Resolute Dawn](../characters/mara-vale.md). At the owner’s direction, she is now Mira Dalen, and her family surname is Dalen. No relationship to the captain is established.
- Mira calls Elin her granddaughter and identifies Tomas as her father. At the owner’s direction, his relationship to Elin was corrected from “great-great-grandfather” to “great-grandfather”; the earlier wording is preserved in the source editions.
- The source draft contains section headings and exceeds the current 6,000-word guideline for new standalone side stories. It is retained as requested for historical-lore and narration review without an unapproved prose cut.
- The source conversation's original postscript labeled the lore proposed. The owner's later direction to add it as historical lore supersedes that status label; the preserved source still records it for provenance.
