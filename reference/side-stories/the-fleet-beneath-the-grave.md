# The Fleet Beneath the Grave — proposed standalone story reference

Source: [owner-supplied revised story](../../side-stories/the-fleet-beneath-the-grave.md), 29 October 2026. Full-story spoilers. Events are proposed standalone continuity and do not establish a main-novel timeline.

Marcus Delgado captains the salvage vessel Second Chance with engineer Iona Pell, navigator Nessa Varn, and temporary contractors Joel Hart and Anton Berg (Joel and Anton von Hjertstedt). An accidental purchase brings them to Kessarin Fleet Support Annex, where the custodial intelligence Castellan guards forty ancient military hulls and the records of 619 people who died aboard an evacuation transport under a compromised order. The text describes a Varanthi-era background.

The Grey Tally and the Voss Salvage Cartel contest the salvage; later Legate Cassian Veyr and Tribune Mara Kelt lead a Zenith integration attempt. House Captain Hald Rynn commands the frigates Vowkeeper and Grey Lantern alongside a Sanctuary tender. Iona dies after helping disable the military network; the Second Chance escapes with four of its five crew. A later independent inquiry retains restricted custody of surviving structures, while Marcus's compensation claim remains unresolved. The historical archive preserves the evacuation victims' records. These events do not establish that Zenith's wider fleet or government was defeated.

The scene VI count of assault craft merits editorial clarification: the initial division of twelve craft into four, four and four and the loss of two is followed by a report of nine approach contacts and three support craft, without stating whether these counts refer to the same surviving group.
