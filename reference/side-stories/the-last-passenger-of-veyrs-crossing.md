# The Last Passenger of Veyr's Crossing — story reference

**Scope:** Owner-authorized editorial revision, 8 November 2026. Proposed standalone continuity; no established placement in the main novel. Full-story spoilers follow. The cause of the events is unresolved.

- **House ship and crew:** HVS *Skymning*, a reconnaissance frigate of 86 personnel, commanded by Captain Mara Teld; Commander Elias Vorn; sensor officer Lieutenant Sera Kald; Chief Engineer Tovan Rell; Justicar Haldane; medic Lieutenant Nerin.
- **Missing vessel:** *Mourning Star*, Veyr Transit Cooperative passenger transport, recorded as departing 73 years earlier with 12,418 registered passengers and 293 crew. Signs of continued power, maintenance and stationary figures remain unexplained.
- **Location and mission:** Veyr's Crossing, a damaged Varanthi relay investigated after unauthorized authentication attempts and three merchant disappearances. No connection between the disappearances and the transport is established.
- **Rescue and testimony:** A caller identifying herself as Lyra says the ship departed yesterday and seeks her brother Aren. The crew rescues Lyra; a man apparently matching Aren is seen, but attempts to reach him fail before the ship disappears from their sensors. His condition and the fate of others remain unknown. Independent beacons and courier reports preserve evidence for a recovery effort. The closing recording contains knocks of uncertain origin.
