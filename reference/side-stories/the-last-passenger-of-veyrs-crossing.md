# The Last Passenger of Veyr's Crossing — story reference

**Scope:** Owner-supplied text submitted for review on 8 November 2026. Proposed standalone continuity; no established placement in the main novel. Full-story spoilers follow. The text stops at Lyra's transmission; its mysteries and outcome remain unresolved.

- **House ship and crew:** HVS *Skymning*, a reconnaissance frigate of 86 personnel, commanded by Captain Mara Teld; Commander Elias Vorn; sensor officer Lieutenant Sera Kald; Chief Engineer Tovan Rell; Justicar Haldane.
- **Missing vessel:** *Mourning Star*, Veyr Transit Cooperative passenger transport, recorded as departing 73 years earlier with 12,418 registered passengers and 293 crew. Crew find apparent signs of continued power, maintenance, and stationary figures.
- **Location and mission:** Veyr's Crossing, a damaged Varanthi relay, investigated after unauthorized authentication attempts and three merchant disappearances. A connection between those disappearances and the transport is not established.
- **Unverified testimony:** A caller identifying herself as Lyra says the ship departed yesterday. Her identity and the cause of the discrepancy are not confirmed.
