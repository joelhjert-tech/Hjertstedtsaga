# The Kharad Trial — story continuity

Source: [accepted side story](../../side-stories/the-kharad-trial.md), revised under owner authorization on 26 October 2026. **Full-story spoilers.** These facts belong to this standalone prose story. They do not activate comic identities, armour, ships or environments.

## Setting and authority

The Kharad here is the Talyrn catastrophe-intervention institution, not the unrelated Kharad War Clans. The story takes place on Vaerith in the Odrava. Its relation to the main novel and renewed contact through Ardenvast is unspecified.

Processing Complex Nine suffered a quarantine incident ninety-three years before the story. A regional site-transfer record omitted the quarantine annex; a training exclusion zone was subsequently maintained above it. The later incident exposes that failure, suspends certification and triggers independent review of the training authority. Only the western grounds reopen under new certification.

## Supporting cast

- **Nara Veyl:** candidate group leader; left-arm injury during escape; all five candidates survive. Later depicted as an instructor. Her younger sister is elsewhere on Vaerith and is never established as captured.
- **Jarek Solven:** mechanic and scoreboard keeper; unsafe diagnostic connection helps release a containment lock. Electrical burns affect both hands before he works on later doors.
- **Bren Taval:** candidate; two fingers crushed at a door wheel and subsequently lost. His proposed solo stand is challenged; his willingness to protect teammates remains valuable.
- **Lysa Orven:** medical specialist; repeatedly questions unreliable evidence. Chemical lung damage follows a damaged respirator coupling during the outside crossing.
- **Taren Vos:** route-finding and monitoring; identifies the drain exit, operates an isolated recovery verifier and later receives stress-injury treatment.
- **Commandant Reth Varun:** local training commandant; orders recovery and containment response, assesses the team and includes institutional failings in the review. Not established as leader of the Continuance.
- **Instructor Maelis:** supports the command response and archive checks.
- **Lieutenant Arel Dov:** recovery officer whose ninety-three-year-old distress recording is reused. Listed dead and unrecovered.

## Incident and consequences

The organism uses old communications hardware and archive credentials. Its apparent suit telemetry is unreliable. Possible victim awareness and the reproduction of Nara's sister remain unresolved; this story does not establish telepathy or universal access to any secure system.

The team closes a real ventilation feed, then makes an unsafe further investigation of the refuge access. A fresh challenge checked by an isolated recovery verifier and a matching visual craft signal establish their extraction route. This procedure is local evidence, not a claim that Talyrn technology is immune to compromise.

The shelter locator remains behind. The extraction team supplies a separate landing beacon. On the final cable, the order is Taren, Nara, Bren, Lysa and Jarek. A fall brings the candidates together before Nara cuts behind Jarek, leaving all five on the safe side.

All five candidates survive with lasting injuries; the incident kills eleven other people. The scoreboard truthfully records five returned and deductions still being counted. The final monitoring failure leaves the organism's survival unresolved.
