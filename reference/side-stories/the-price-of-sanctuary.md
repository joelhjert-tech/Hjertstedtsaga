# The Price of Sanctuary — story reference

Scope: owner-supplied opening installment submitted 8 November 2026 for review; proposed standalone continuity only, chronology unestablished. Full spoilers. Claims are attributed to this story, not established main-novel canon.

- Captain Varek Thenn commands the damaged cruiser *Enduring River* and seven surviving Veyran vessels, including the medical ship *Mercy of Rain*. First Officer Audren Vash accompanies him.
- The Veyran survivors reach Sol after eleven years of war with the Sorynth Veil. Their flotilla lost forty-three vessels; a separate civilian convoy escaped Damaris Reach, with its later fate unknown.
- Terran Commodore Elias Rourke directs emergency assistance and armed custody; yardmaster Helena Ström oversees work at Martian Naval Repair Anchorage Seventeen. Medical officer Nessa Calder and rescue-tug lieutenant Mara Keene assist.
- Ström and a Federal analyst question whether recurring Sorynth signals were understood correctly. Their meaning, the missing people’s fate, and Sorynth intentions remain unresolved.
- The story names a Gate of Truce and Neptune Gate Authority; the published [Pluto Gate](../gates-and-lanes/pluto-gate.md) profile places the Sol gateway beyond Pluto. Their relationship is unestablished. [Damaris](../locations/damaris.md) is also a named novel location; whether Damaris Reach is related is unestablished. [Sanctuary](../fleet-classes/house-sanctuary-1.md) is an established House tender designation; the story's medical transport relationship is unspecified.
