# The Price of Sanctuary — story reference

Scope: owner-authorized revision of the opening installment, 8 November 2026; proposed standalone continuity only, chronology unestablished. Full spoilers. Claims are attributed to this story, not established main-novel canon.

- Captain Varek Thenn commands the damaged cruiser *Enduring River* and seven surviving Veyran vessels, including the medical ship *Mercy of Rain*. First Officer Audren Vash accompanies him. Chief engineer Pell works with Terran rescuers to protect a reactor cooling loop.
- The Veyran survivors reach Sol after eleven years of war with the Sorynth Veil. Their flotilla lost forty-three vessels; a separate civilian convoy escaped Damaris Reach, with its later fate unknown.
- Terran Commodore Elias Rourke directs emergency assistance and armed custody; yardmaster Helena Ström oversees work at Martian Naval Repair Anchorage Seventeen. Medical officer Nessa Calder and rescue-tug lieutenant Mara Keene assist.
- The revised opening identifies the [Pluto Gate](../gates-and-lanes/pluto-gate.md) as the arrival point and Gate of Truce as the Veyran chart name. [Damaris](../locations/damaris.md) is a named novel location; whether Damaris Reach is related is unestablished.
- Veyran officers had tried retransmitting a Sorynth pulse, with an inconclusive result. Ström and a Federal analyst question the signals' interpretation; the analyst identifies a possible response in a first-contact recording. The signal's meaning, the missing people's fate, and Sorynth intentions remain unresolved.
