# The Silence Beneath Shardveil — proposed standalone story reference

Source: [owner-supplied story](../../side-stories/the-silence-beneath-shardveil.md), 3 November 2026. Full-story spoilers. The story is proposed standalone continuity; its placement relative to the main novel is not established.

Caldrathi Commander Vexthor observes a House operation against the Zathine Corsair Syndicate in the Shardveil Nebula. Captain Nira Khan leads a six-person forward cell: four armoured Hunters (Khan, Garrik Dane, Yara Venn and Orren Pike) and specialists Eda Moren and Lio Fen. Dennis Rask coordinates containment; Annette von Hjertstedt oversees judicial and prisoner protections. The cell disables three command relays, opening an approach for House warships and rescue teams. Orren dies holding an access route; Doctor Sareth Vale, a Seraph command host, is taken into custody.

The detention archive accounts for 143 people from civilian vessels: 31 dead before the operation, 16 transferred elsewhere, and 96 alive at the facility; five of those 96 die during the operation and 91 are extracted alive. This is the archive's accounting, not necessarily a count of everyone aboard the station.

Editorial review: the source leaves Moss's eventual custody unreported, the pilot's identity unspecified, Eda's injury and the Black Needle's damage unshown, and the distinction between the 16 transferred people and 16 visible hostages unclear. The story calls four Hunters a Lance; Chapter 3 Scene 11 assigns twelve suits to Duskbringer, so formation terminology warrants clarification without assuming all Lances are fixed-size. The story remains exactly as supplied pending an accepted revision.
