# The Silence Beneath Shardveil — proposed standalone story reference

Source: [owner-authorized revision](../../side-stories/the-silence-beneath-shardveil.md), 3 November 2026; [original owner-supplied edition](../../sources/editions/the-silence-beneath-shardveil-owner-supplied-2026-11-03.md). Full-story spoilers. The story is proposed standalone continuity; its placement relative to the main novel is not established.

Caldrathi Commander Vexthor observes a House operation against the Zathine Corsair Syndicate in the Shardveil Nebula. Captain Nira Khan leads a six-person forward cell: four armoured Hunters (Khan, Garrik Dane, Yara Venn and Orren Pike), a detachment from a larger Lance, and specialists Eda Moren and Lio Fen. Dennis Rask coordinates containment; Annette von Hjertstedt oversees judicial and prisoner protections. The cell disables three command relays, opening an approach for House warships and rescue teams. Orren dies holding an access route; Doctor Sareth Vale, a Seraph command host, is taken into custody.

The detention archive accounts for 143 people from civilian vessels: 31 dead before the operation, 16 transferred elsewhere, and 96 alive at the facility; five of those 96 die during the operation and 91 are extracted alive. This is the archive's accounting, not necessarily a count of everyone aboard the station.

In the revision, Eda pilots Black Needle and recalls it remotely; Moss is medically monitored and transferred separately. Sixteen hostages at the command chamber are among the 96 located alive; the 16 transferred before the attack are a separate group. Vexthor passes an intermittent contact bearing to the House, which independently identifies its target. Orren's and Eda's earlier exchange adds personal context to his loss. The story remains proposed standalone continuity.
