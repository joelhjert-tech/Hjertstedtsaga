# The Seventh Lock — story reference

**Full-story spoilers.** This reference is limited to the [restored longer standalone story](../../side-stories/the-seventh-lock.md); its position in main-novel continuity remains proposed.

The 43-kilometer Nharavi worldship *Aurum*, carrying 286,117 people at the opening, flees a Zenith formation. Captain Ithrel, First Officer Sereth, and human engineers Rosa Danner, Tom Brant and May confront the sealed transit custodian Sael. Its records show that Nharavi authorities overrode safety warnings during the Oren transit; forty million died. Elder Vorin admits helping disable a safety system. Rosa and the team restore independent safety authority before the Lanth Spur–Merien crossing. During the assault, Security Commander Relan Voss dies holding a barrier for 800 evacuees; Nemi Vareth and technician Halen Orith are among the other losses.

Merien is a House naval yard. Commodore Ragna Sved commands its defense, and Captain Astrid Nyholm commands the gate-guarding dreadnought HVS *Winter's Anvil*. All five Zenith vessels follow *Aurum* through the gate. After Zenith attacks, High Marshal Joel orders the destruction of hostile vessels continuing resistance. The dreadnought destroys the five hulls; House forces recover survivors. HVS *Pale Watch* and Captain Edda Toren are lost while protecting the worldship. Six months later *Aurum* remains at Merien, and the Oren archives are under Nharavi civilian authority for independent investigation.

The story introduces *Winter's Anvil* as an orbital dreadnought; it does not establish it as the atmospheric Frostbastion or a House Titan. The machine Sael in this story is not identified as the published character Sael-Vareth.

## Carrier craft accounting

The carrier commits 18 boarding craft at Lanth Spur, followed by its six remaining boarding craft. At Merien it launches 28 unmanned attack drones from separate racks; these are not the boarding craft. This owner-requested correction resolves the earlier unexplained count. The earlier ending in which Zenith remained on the far side of the gate is superseded by the owner's selected ending.
