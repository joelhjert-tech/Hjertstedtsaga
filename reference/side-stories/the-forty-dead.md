# The Forty Dead — story reference

Source: [owner-authorized revision](../../side-stories/the-forty-dead.md), 8 November 2026; [original edition](../../sources/editions/the-forty-dead-original-2026-11-08.md). Full-story spoilers. **Proposed standalone continuity**; placement relative to the main novel and other stories remains unestablished.

After Terran clearance at the Pluto Gate, Commodore Helena Varek oversees the rebuilding of forty condemned Imperial warships. Six hulls are dismantled; thirty-four return to service using Sol-built systems. Yard engineer Lena Ortiz dies during the work and is remembered by Helena. Chief structural engineer Tomas Rell identifies a viable frame; Commander Elias Dorn commands *TFP Resolute*, which protects a convoy at a cost of three sailors. Legate Severian Kord later reviews the rebuilt fleet. The story proposes that the program contributes knowledge for a new Terran heavy warship.

The revision gives this standalone account a specific charter and transfer-agreement dispute, parallel reconstruction across Sol's facilities, and a clearer cost to maintenance capacity. Those details and the story's broader implications are not established as main-novel canon. Repository storage and this authorized prose revision do not approve audio, artwork, comic or other publication.
