# The Forty Dead — story reference

Source: [owner-supplied story](../../side-stories/the-forty-dead.md), 8 November 2026. Full-story spoilers. The submitted text is preserved as supplied while review suggestions await the owner's decisions. **Proposed standalone continuity**; placement relative to the main novel and other stories remains unestablished.

Commodore Helena Varek oversees the Terran rebuilding of forty condemned Imperial warships. Six hulls are dismantled; thirty-four return to service using Sol-built systems. Chief structural engineer Tomas Rell identifies a viable frame; Commander Elias Dorn commands *TFP Resolute*, which protects a convoy at a cost of three sailors. Legate Severian Kord later reviews the rebuilt fleet. The story proposes that the program contributes knowledge for a new Terran heavy warship.

The source describes fleet numbers, timings and political arrangements from this standalone account; its wider implications, chronology and the opening's Earth/Jupiter geography remain subject to review. This reference does not establish the story as main-novel canon or approve an editorial revision, audio, artwork, comic or other publication.
