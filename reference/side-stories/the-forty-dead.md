# The Forty Dead — story reference

Source: [owner-authorized remake](../../side-stories/the-forty-dead.md), 8 November 2026; [prior revision](../../sources/editions/the-forty-dead-revision-2026-11-08.md) and [original edition](../../sources/editions/the-forty-dead-original-2026-11-08.md). Full-story spoilers. **Proposed standalone continuity**; placement relative to the main novel and other stories remains unestablished.

After Terran clearance at the Pluto Gate, forty condemned Imperial warships arrive at Jupiter. A tow failure threatens a yard gantry, where engineer Lena Ortiz helps rescue trapped workers. Commodore Helena Varek oversees the rebuilding program; chief structural engineer Tomas Rell and Lena discover that Hull Seventeen's frame can support a new ship. Lena dies in a later machinery accident despite a rescue attempt. Tomas keeps her annotated survey as the work continues.

Helena faces legal pressure from Legate Severian Kord, industrial constraints and hazardous trials. Six unsafe hulls are dismantled; thirty-four return to service using Sol-built systems. Hull Seventeen becomes *TFP Resolute*, commanded by Elias Dorn. The cruiser protects an industrial convoy against three raider frigates; forty-seven people aboard the convoy die before it arrives, and three Terran sailors die aboard *Resolute*. Kord later reviews the rebuilt fleet. The story proposes that the program contributes knowledge for a new Terran heavy warship.

The action-led remake expands the arrival, Lena's role and rescue attempt, reactor trial, political opposition and convoy engagement. These events and the account's broader implications are proposed standalone continuity, not established main-novel events. The previously generated Snowball audiobook follows the earlier revision, not this remake.
