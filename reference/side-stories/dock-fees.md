# Dock Fees — story reference

**Scope: full-story spoilers.** The [complete standalone story](../../side-stories/dock-fees.md) is proposed standalone continuity. Its placement relative to the main novel is unestablished.

Tern is an independent repair station on a busy freight route, with four berths and three tugs. Superintendent Tamsin Korr manages the station; Ivo is its chief mechanic and Sella is a tug pilot. An unnamed Directorate of Ash captain requests priority repairs for a damaged warship. Tessa Rell is its chief engineer.

Toxic coolant leaking from the warship threatens Tern's air intake while its clinic is short of medical filters. Ivo and Rell isolate the source, and Sella uses a cargo capture screen while another tug moves the ship away. Intake readings improve, the medical delivery proceeds, and the captain pays for repairs and related costs.

The owner directed the superintendent's rename from Mara Venn to Tamsin Korr to distinguish her from Captain Mara Venn in *The Unfired Salvo*. The name correction does not establish any connection between those characters. [Source record](../../sources/dock-fees-2026-11-12.json).
