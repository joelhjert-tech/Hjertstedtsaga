# The Names Beneath the Iron — story reference

Source: [owner-authorized revision](../../side-stories/the-names-beneath-the-iron.md), 4 November 2026. Full-story spoilers. Its wider relationship to the main novel is proposed; the hearing is after renewed Talyrn contact.

At Haldane Research Ring, HVS Last Lantern and a civilian dockmaster organize an evacuation by cargo sled and barge. The cutter supplies and tows the barge; 312 civilians are brought out on 31 loaded trips. House crew members Leva Orn, Joren Pell and Mara Kel die, as do civilians including teacher Lessa Vey and dockmaster Varek Oss. Fifteen years later Warrant Sergeant Brenwick recounts the rescue at a hearing on emergency access, carrying survivors' marks and names of the dead on his old armor and attached memorial leaves.

Other named characters include Captain Yara Vell; Kesh delegate Dravik and his nephew Tavo; and Orani delegate Ismera. Joel and Annette von Hjertstedt attend. Cutter logs, including suit-camera footage, open channels and sled manifests, support the evidence. The proposed blanket prohibition fails; several delegations endorse a narrower agreement.
