# The Names Beneath the Iron — story reference

Source: [owner-supplied story](../../side-stories/the-names-beneath-the-iron.md), 4 November 2026. Full-story spoilers. At Haldane Research Ring, HVS Last Lantern and a civilian dockmaster organize an evacuation by cargo sled and barge: 312 survivors cross in 31 trips. House crew members Leva Orn, Joren Pell and Mara Kel die, as do civilians including teacher Lessa Vey and dockmaster Varek Oss. Fifteen years later Warrant Sergeant Brenwick recounts the rescue at a hearing on emergency access, carrying survivors' marks and names of the dead on his old armor.

Other named characters include Captain Yara Vell; Kesh delegate Dravik and his nephew Tavo; and Orani delegate Ismera. Joel and Annette von Hjertstedt attend the hearing. The proposed blanket prohibition fails; several delegations endorse a narrower agreement.
