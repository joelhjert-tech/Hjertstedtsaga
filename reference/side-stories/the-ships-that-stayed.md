# The Ships That Stayed — story reference

**Scope: full-story spoilers.** The [complete standalone story](../../side-stories/the-ships-that-stayed.md) is proposed standalone continuity; its placement relative to the main novel is unestablished.

Garooth raiders attack the mining world of Bracken and threaten its repair yard. Veteran captain Ingrid Vale takes the old frigate Northbound to protect evacuation traffic. Yardmaster Orin Pell coordinates the yard; engineer Toman manages the frigate's damaged systems; Captain Soren commands the escort Lantern's Wake.

On the surface, miner and mechanic Lysa Marr, truck driver Anik, House lieutenant Halen and local security officer Deren help keep the mountain road open. Deren dies protecting the withdrawal. Distress transmissions, some recorded and replayed, heighten uncertainty and psychological strain. The last shuttles escape; Lantern's Wake breaks apart after rescuing evacuees, and Soren is missing. The boy's father remains unaccounted for. Survivors record the dead and missing. The patrol and the later fate of Bracken are not resolved in the story.

These names, ships, places and events are facts of this standalone story; their connection to other stories is not established. [Source record](../../sources/ships-that-stayed-2026-11-12.json).
