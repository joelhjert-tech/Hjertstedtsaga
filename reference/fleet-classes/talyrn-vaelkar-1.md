The Talyrn Continuance

Give the formation a heavy protected center.

Strength creates obligation. An isolated human commonwealth survives through mutual support, chosen communities and fleets built to keep functioning after catastrophe.

Overlapping armor, defensive coverage and several protected command positions. Do not make a ceremonial bridge its identifying feature.

Lower acceleration. It cannot chase lighter formations or make the whole fleet invulnerable.
