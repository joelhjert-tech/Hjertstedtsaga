The Talyrn Continuance

Keep the fleet repaired, supplied and alive.

Strength creates obligation. An isolated human commonwealth survives through mutual support, chosen communities and fleets built to keep functioning after catastrophe.

Repair, fabrication, medicine and ammunition support. Proposed external work equipment must remain functional and retractable.

Limited independent combat ability. Supplies, time and recoverable damage constrain what it can restore.
