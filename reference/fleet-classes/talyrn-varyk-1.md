The Talyrn Continuance

Protect the ships beside it.

Strength creates obligation. An isolated human commonwealth survives through mutual support, chosen communities and fleets built to keep functioning after catastrophe.

Point-defense coverage and protected electronic-warfare arrays dominate. Avoid a destroyer-sized forward weapon as the focal point.

Moderate offensive weight. Its value falls if deployed as an isolated heavy attacker.
