## Mandatory visual identity

The Splitfang must look **more advanced and considerably slimmer** than the same class of ship in regular human, Garooth or Talyrn fleets.

Imagine an elongated black-metal dagger with a forked nose. The long spine carries most engineering and weapons along a tight central axis. Fine, overlapping hull plates resemble the Vharok's dark scales without making the ship biological. Sensor slits and targeting apertures glow amber only when active. Propulsion is carried in a small number of tightly recessed blue-white engine ports at the rear.

Its dorsal surface stays **low and clean**. Missile cells and boarding slots remain closed until needed. There are no large dorsal castles, broad wings, scrap towers, oversized cathedral features or swollen mid-hull hangars. It should feel **precision-made, expensive, fast and predatory**.

The split prow creates the recognizable distant sensor silhouette associated with a “Split Hull.” Two narrow blade-like prongs flank a central sensor/weapon channel; they are not literal teeth.

## Typical action

A Splitfang approaches under passive sensing, forces an escort to turn, then places a disabling shot into propulsion while another hunter prevents escape. It stays mobile and off-axis rather than trading repeated broadsides with a heavier opponent. At Knife Range, launch hatches deploy boarding cutters after the prey's defenses have been suppressed.

The frigate is not a prison barge. Captives are passed to protected Mawhold carriers and tenders, because taking prisoners is a logistics operation as well as an assault.

## Production-art guardrails

**Must show:** narrow beam, long sleek spear-like hull, subtle split nose, graphite and dark silver finish, tiny amber sensor accents, recessed blue-white drives, refined industrial construction.

**Must avoid:** broad bulky conventional hulls, living flesh-ships, Seraph religious spires, Garooth welded plates, Sorynth pale crescents, glowing ornament everywhere, visible human-style bridge windows.

**Scale caution:** the SVG is a **shape study only**. No official dimensions, weapon count, transit performance or comparative tonnage are yet approved.
