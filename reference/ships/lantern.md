## Role and service

Lantern is a working passenger liner built around protected access and habitable space. Recessed observation lounges and small cabin windows offer views without turning the pressure hull into an exposed glass shell. Passenger docking and luggage handling follow separate paths.

## Recognising the hull

Pearl-grey plating, a dark pressure keel, muted copper trim and terraced upper lounges distinguish the liner.

## Operational limits

Passenger service depends on protected lanes, available docking access and a reliable maintenance schedule.
