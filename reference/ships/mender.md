# Mender recovery tender

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/ships/mender

Verdant Iron Compact

Recover damaged craft and support repairs away from a fixed yard.

## Role and service

Mender brings a working cradle to the casualty. Its open repair frame, enclosed fabrication spaces and articulated handling arms allow crews to secure a damaged hull before repairs begin. Protected service cassettes and copper pipe housings make the machinery accessible without leaving biological systems exposed to vacuum.

## Recognising the hull

An open horseshoe working frame, offset workshop modules and folded handling arms identify the tender.

## Operational limits

Mender has little independent combat capability and needs escorts or a secure recovery area.
