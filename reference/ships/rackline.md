## Role and service

Rackline makes its cargo visible: detachable modules sit along an exposed structural spine, with a small offset crew cabin kept clear of the handling path. Common couplings let a working port exchange loads without rebuilding the vessel around each contract.

## Recognising the hull

A narrow spine, staggered cargo modules and a transverse aft machinery beam distinguish the hauler.

## Operational limits

External cargo and clamp fittings require protected dock handling; the ship depends on safe approaches.
