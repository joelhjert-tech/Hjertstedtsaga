Verdant Iron Compact

Endurance and convoy-defence dreadnought.

## Role and service

Patient Thunder is built to stay with the convoy. A low central pressure citadel, segmented armour and independently enclosed machinery spaces distribute the consequences of damage. Protected service trunks and replaceable cassettes support a long working life, but endurance still requires repair crews and regular supply.

## Recognising the hull

A broad low lobed hull with patinated-green plates, pale service cassettes and enclosed copper trunks.

## Operational limits

Low acceleration and substantial maintenance demand; survival does not make it invulnerable.
