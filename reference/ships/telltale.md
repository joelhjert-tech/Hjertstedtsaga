## Role and service

Telltale is an uncrewed instrument platform. Folding sensor booms and separated optical apertures gather observations from a compact service core, while small attitude-control nozzles orient the payload. There is no cabin, passenger space or defensive armament.

## Recognising the hull

A squat hexagonal instrument core, unequal folding booms and ceramic service tiles distinguish the probe.

## Operational limits

The probe cannot defend itself. Its observations still require verification and do not establish unquestionable sensor truth.
