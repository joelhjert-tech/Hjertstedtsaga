## Role and service

Farwatch carries a small crew in a protected central compartment behind a narrow sensor snout. Short sensor shoulders, a retractable mast and a closed probe bay support cautious observation. The hull is designed to inspect, report and withdraw rather than win a fleet engagement.

## Recognising the hull

A low deep body, blunt sensor snout, short shoulders and offset aft drives carry porcelain-white and cobalt panels.

## Operational limits

Survival depends on caution, verified approaches and an escape route; limited combat power constrains independent action.
