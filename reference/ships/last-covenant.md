Throne Imperium

Siege and battle-line anchor.

## Role and service

Last Covenant gives a Throne battle line a protected centre around which escorts can organise. Its broad bastion bow and recessed batteries favour sustained pressure against a defended approach. The ship commits the fleet to holding ground in space; its value depends on the vessels and supply chain that allow it to remain there.

## Recognising the hull

A broad transverse fortress bow, low command terraces, ivory slabs and burgundy shoulder blocks.

## Operational limits

Slow to reposition; relies on escorts to protect its flanks.
