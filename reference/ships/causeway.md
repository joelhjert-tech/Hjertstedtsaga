# Causeway troop lander

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/ships/causeway

Snapshot: new-ships-2026-10-10-v01

Terran Federal Protectorate of Sol

Deliver troops through a controlled approach to a secured landing site.

## Role and service

Causeway carries its troops inside a protected pressure compartment. Compact vector-thrust blocks and sturdy landing feet support a short, broad hull, while a rear deployment ramp gives the unit a clear route out after touchdown. Side hatches provide additional evacuation access.

## Recognising the hull

A flattened hexagonal body, blunt pressure nose and close-set thrust blocks carry navy-grey and off-white armour.

## Operational limits

The lander needs a secured approach and sufficient ground clearance; armour cannot compensate for an exposed landing zone.
