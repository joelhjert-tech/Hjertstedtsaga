# Seraph Throne

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/factions/seraph-throne

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Fleet doctrine · society · ground forces

Ancient parasites using human hosts to seize identities, command structures and control of the gate network.

Faction reference · wider setting lore; not a record of events in every published chapter.

The Seraph are parasites. Their rulers seize living human hosts and carry accumulated memory across generations. The human body is the host; the Seraph intelligence controls it. Humanity is both a rival for old infrastructure and a source of bodies to occupy.

Possession offers more than a captured soldier: a governor’s face, an admiral’s authority or a gatewright’s knowledge can open access that a fleet cannot easily force. Their empire seeks to rule human civilization through its captured leadership.

The High Seraph stands above the Throne Lords and Flesh Ministries. Host selection, conversion and control are organized parts of imperial government.

Vassal Legions include collaborators and conquered auxiliaries. Husk Guard are former hosts or ruined bodies rebuilt into sealed warforms for elite assaults; the Choir of Silence specializes in infiltration and capture. Not every human serving the Throne is necessarily possessed.

Their fleets preserve ancient cores inside repeatedly rebuilt hulls. Throne Carriers serve as mobile courts and host-processing centres; Ascendant battleships anchor sieges, Censer cruisers disrupt command and relays, and Coffin barges carry assault forces. Host vaults and conversion logistics are part of the war machine.

Weathered bone-coloured armour over dark mechanisms defines the Husk Guard direction. Seraph ships pair black ribbed hulls and cathedral-like spires with tarnished metallic detail and restrained red apertures.

These are wider-setting capabilities. They do not establish that every unexplained enemy or buyer in an earlier chapter has already been identified as Seraph.

- **Nature:** Parasites using human hosts
- **Primary objective:** Possession and subjugation
- **Elite assault formation:** Husk Guard
