# Vardos Coalition

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/factions/vardos-coalition

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Fleet doctrine · society · ground forces

A Coalition force whose recovery fleets and boarding troops bring a direct threat to Kestrel.

Faction reference · wider setting lore; not a record of events in every published chapter.

The Coalition expedition at Kestrel seeks control through armed pressure and boarding. Tal’s eventual defeat does not establish the fall of the entire Vardos frontier.

Captured and surrendering personnel remain people under protection. Tal survives capture in the published sequence.

These sheets preserve the established Coalition hull and armour references. Fleet roles distinguish patrol, line combat, troop delivery and recovery; infantry, heavy boarders and command guards carry separate equipment.
