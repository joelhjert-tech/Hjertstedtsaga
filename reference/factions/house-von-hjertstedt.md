# House von Hjertstedt

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/factions/house-von-hjertstedt

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Fleet doctrine · society · ground forces

A sovereign mercenary House whose fleets protect a hidden civilization. Its power rests on conduct, civilian protection and the industrial depth of Vintergard.

Faction reference · wider setting lore; not a record of events in every published chapter.

The House is known across the Reach for Banners, Justicars, disciplined boarding forces and the Charter of Steel. Its obligations include protecting civilians, respecting surrender, punishing predation and preferring seizure when destruction is unnecessary.

The hereditary High Marshal holds final strategic authority, supported by institutions with distinct responsibilities. Justicars enforce the legal and Charter structure; Banner Captains command formations. Civil administrators govern ordinary life. Billions of citizens are not personal servants of the ruling family.

Vintergard is the ancestral sovereign homeland: eight major worlds, extensive habitats and several billion permanent residents. Its population includes sworn families, engineers, farmers, teachers, veterans and refugees granted sanctuary. The military exists to protect that civilization.

The Great Yards around Valdyr form a distributed shipbuilding and repair network. Reserve hulls, refits, trained personnel and logistics explain how damaged Banners can regain strength. Destroyed ships and dead crews remain permanent losses.

Operational Banners serve on contracts, patrols, escorts and frontier assignments throughout the Reach. Home defence, training units, reserves and refitting vessels remain in Vintergard. The entire navy is not gathered at home.

Kestrel accepts voluntary strategic affiliation while Sera retains local command. Ardent’s later Excision and permanent strategic control are a different response to a different threat. These later developments do not rewrite the limits of the earlier prisoner-custody investigation.

The House was built to survive without conquering an empire. Its growing external responsibilities create a difficult question: when does permanent protection become imperial rule?

Hrafn Spear is the House fast troop transport that delivers two hundred elite troops to Kestrel’s medical-sector military collar during Chapter 1.

Sanctuary Specialists are among the most augmented people in House service. Summoned for apparently hopeless cases and exceptional care, they receive extraordinary protection: secured approaches, guarded treatment spaces and a maintained extraction route. They complement the wider medical network rather than replace ordinary medics.

- **Homeland:** Vintergard
- **Civilian capital:** Vinterkrona, Vinterholm
- **Legal foundation:** The Charter of Steel
- **Strategic strength:** Distributed industry, reserves and repair
