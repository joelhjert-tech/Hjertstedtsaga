Bitte von Hjertstedt is Lars’s wife, distinguished by her blonde bob, dark glasses and black ceremonial armor beneath a deep crimson mantle.

Her approved appearance is that of a mature woman with a composed expression, a blonde bob with fringe and dark rectangular glasses. The formal armor has a high collar, articulated gauntlets, aged brass detailing and a long layered skirt over protective boots.

Bitte’s cybernetic details run along her right temple, cheek, ear and neck. Her hair frames the implants, allowing the combination of face, glasses and machinery to remain recognizable.

Her ceremonial clothing shares Lars von Hjertstedt’s restrained black, crimson and aged-metal palette. The matching presentation connects their designs while retaining Bitte’s own face, bearing and armor silhouette.
