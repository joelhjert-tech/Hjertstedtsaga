# Sera Venn

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/characters/sera-venn

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Captain of the Wayward Star · Commander of Kestrel

Sera accepts responsibility for Kestrel’s people before learning the truth about her passengers. Her command remains local as the station chooses House affiliation.

Novel continuity through Chapter 02, Scene 14. Contains spoilers beyond comic Issue 01.

After rescuing civilians, Sera reaches Terminus Spire with a damaged frigate, twenty-eight surviving crew and two crew members already lost. An unpaid contract becomes a thirty-day claim to Sector Command Hub Kestrel.

Kestrel is abandoned by its former authorities, not empty. Halden Rook and the remaining garrison have kept its civilian population alive. Sera’s protection extends to everyone, including those who decline to sign her contract.

Joel and Anton reveal their House identities during the crisis. Sera does not change clothes or surrender command at that revelation. When reinforcements arrive, she authorizes the defence of the station’s people.

Formal House affiliation comes after the battle, through voluntary acceptance. Kestrel and the Wayward Star keep Sera’s command. Chapter 02 opens with restoration and a new clean station-command coat whose local Kestrel identity remains primary.

- **Command:** Wayward Star and Kestrel
- **Affiliation:** Voluntary House protection
- **Principle:** Protection includes non-signers
- **First appearance:** Chapter 01 · Opening
