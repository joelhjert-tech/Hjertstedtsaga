# Marek Soren

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/characters/marek-soren

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Former Acting Director of Vardos Coalition intelligence

Marek Soren is a restrained intelligence director whose knowledge of covert operations makes him more dangerous—and more valuable—than his helpless appearance suggests.

Published snapshot; may contain spoilers for the former eight-chapter edition. Chapter 8 was removed from the current main novel on 8 November 2026; its events are not part of the current main-novel edition.

Introduced as Holt’s prisoner, Soren describes his former responsibility for covert operations, warrants and payments. He knows how to make information change the behaviour of a room. Even in a collar and cuffs he can expose risk, bargain for attention and complicate his captor’s contract.

His transport becomes part of the Ardent investigation. His usefulness as an information source must be kept separate from innocence or reliability. Records, testimony and what the other characters have actually learned should govern each revelation.

Garran Holt: warrant carrier controlling his transfer.

Mara Vale: captain responsible for the escort ship.

House investigators: recipients of evidence, not automatic believers in his claims.

- **Affiliation:** Former Vardos Coalition; in custody
- **Role:** Former Acting Director of Vardos Coalition intelligence
- **Also known as:** Soren

## Appearance — image review, 31 October 2026

The published custody sheet depicts a slim, clean-shaven man with short dark hair greying at the temples. He wears a plain dark high-necked outfit, a restraint collar and linked wrist restraints; these are custody-specific details.
