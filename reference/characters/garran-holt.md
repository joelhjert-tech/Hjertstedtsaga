# Garran Holt

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/characters/garran-holt

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Bounty hunter and warrant carrier

Garran Holt is the professional bounty hunter carrying a live prisoner, a valuable warrant and a contract that becomes much more dangerous than its advertised route.

Contains novel spoilers through Chapter 8.

Holt assembles a mercenary convoy to deliver Marek Soren from Kestrel to Ardent Judicial Relay. The warrant pays for a living prisoner. He keeps control when Soren raises the price of the operation by revealing how much intelligence he carries, rather than striking him to silence the room.

The escort exposes a conflict between the apparent authority of the warrant and the people waiting at its destination. Holt’s part in the story rests on custody, professional obligation and the consequences of discovering what a lawful-looking delivery can conceal.

Marek Soren: prisoner whose knowledge makes the contract dangerous.

Mara Vale: captain hired for the escort.

Joel and Anton: observers and participants who test the delivery’s legitimacy.

- **Affiliation:** Independent contractor
- **Role:** Bounty hunter and warrant carrier
- **Also known as:** Holt
