# Annette von Hjertstedt

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/characters/annette

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

House von Hjertstedt · Lucy and Kira

Annette’s composed command presence is paired with neural interfaces to the advanced AIs Lucy and Kira.

Contains character and novel spoilers.

Annette has blue-grey eyes and long, wavy dark-blonde hair. Her current reference shows a tailored charcoal medical coat with ivory panels, restrained teal trim, utility pouches, black trousers and tall boots.

Two permanent cybernetic horns sit above and behind her temples: short, backward-curving matte-black implants integrated with her skull and nervous system. She deliberately chose their appearance. The current sheet shows the pair exposed and inactive.

The implants house interfaces for Lucy and Kira, supporting encrypted communications, sensor fusion, electronic warfare and a direct neural link to Annette and her flagship. Her hair can conceal them; their appearance here does not indicate activation.

- **Affiliation:** House von Hjertstedt
- **AI interfaces:** Lucy and Kira
- **Implants:** Two permanent cybernetic horns
