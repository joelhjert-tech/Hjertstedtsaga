Lars von Hjertstedt is a member of House von Hjertstedt and Bitte’s husband, associated with the revelation of the House’s hidden strategic strength at Sol.

Vintergard is the concealed homeland behind the House’s fleets. In the Revelation at Sol, Lars discloses enough of its strategic depth to explain how three battered fleets were already being replenished. That resilience rests on industry and reserves; destroyed hulls and lost crews remain real losses.

His formal appearance combines black armor, a high collar, deep crimson drapery and aged metal ornament. A bald head, glasses and a stern, mature face give him a distinct silhouette. Visible cybernetics follow the left temple, cheek and neck.

Lars and Bitte von Hjertstedt share a formal visual language of dark armor and crimson cloth. Their separate character sheets preserve their individual faces, proportions and opposite-side cybernetic details.
