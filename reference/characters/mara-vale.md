# Mara Vale

Source: https://tales-from-the-known-reach.pages.dev/universe/characters/mara-vale

Snapshot: issue03-2026-10-08-v01

Captain of the Resolute Dawn

Mara Vale commands the Resolute Dawn with an eye on crew survival, machinery and the real cost of a contract. A client’s payment does not excuse abandoning people.

Contains novel spoilers through Chapter 8.

Mara accepts Garran Holt’s prisoner escort contract. Her record includes civilian evacuations and a disciplinary complaint for refusing an order to abandon a damaged transport. Joel notices that she watches the prisoner rather than the money, and he and Anton approach her as prospective assault crew.

The convoy draws her into the Ardent investigation and the consequences of Project Shepherd. She remains an experienced captain with her own judgement, not an audience for House power. Later, her practical scrutiny contributes to the smaller-ship proving-ground programme and its insistence on experienced command.

Venn Korda: security chief aboard her ship.

Garran Holt and Marek Soren: escort client and prisoner.

Joel and Anton: recruits whose concealed identities complicate trust.

Elara Vos: another captain involved in the smaller-ship programme.

- **Affiliation:** Independent mercenary command
- **Role:** Captain of the Resolute Dawn
- **Also known as:** Captain Mara, Captain Vale
