Ardent Central is the judicial and administrative heart of Ardent. Its public institutions, civilian docks and inhabited rings serve a population whose daily life continues around the machinery of government.

Formerly the center of Ardent Judicial Authority, Central contains the Special Custody complex whose records expose Project Shepherd. House forces secure the capital and preserve evidence while protecting civilian life. Under House administration, Justicar courts operate openly, screened civil servants return to duty, and markets, schools and traffic resume.

Administrative heart of the Hjertstedt Ardent March. Civilian access, food distribution and medical traffic remain central functions. Defensive collars and a separate outer patrol fortress protect approaches without turning the civic station into Bastion.

The archive chamber shown in this collection belongs to the historical Special Custody setting. It is distinct from the public courts and civic administration. The earlier Ardent Judicial Relay entry follows that part of the story.

Explore the three principal installations: Ardent Central, Ardent Bastion and Ardent Gatehold. The wider territorial record includes the Ardent agricultural world, Ardent mining world, Ardent industrial planet and Ardent restricted administrative colony.
