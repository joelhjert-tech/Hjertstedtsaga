The prisoner escort and Special Custody

An intended lawful handover exposes a hidden trade in people.

Novel continuity through Chapter 02 · includes spoilers.

Garran Holt contracts to escort Marek Soren from Kestrel to Ardent, through Damaris. The real prisoner later travels aboard Mara Vale’s Resolute Dawn while Holt provides a decoy.

Captured attack files implicate the destination itself. At Ardent, Lysandra Kael is captured and the archives preserved. Joel declares an intervention limited to Special Custody, protecting civilian traffic and cooperating with honest officials.

Banner Group Vargskold stays dark: one Oath, four Vigils, two destroyers, one carrier and Sanctuary support. There is no Chapter 02 orbital battle or House annexation of Ardent.

Project Shepherd reveals non-human custody partners without identifying them as the Seraph Throne. Ruined Ardent Gatehold and the later Ash / Seraph storyline are Chapter 03 preview material; their relationship to this station remains to be established by that chapter.

For the broader capital and its civic spaces, visit Ardent Central. Continue through Ardent Bastion and Ardent Gatehold to explore the other principal installations.
