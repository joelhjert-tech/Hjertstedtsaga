Ardent Bastion is the heavy military station responsible for protecting Ardent’s industrial worlds. Its fleet facilities make it the region’s military center and a crucial link between industrial output and deployed force.

Bastion holds the largest concentration of Ardent fleet mass during the campaign. Its capture breaks the old military command. Surrendered personnel are screened, with suitable technical staff returned to work.

A rebuilt fleet fortress with expanded defensive rings, protected docks and permanent Seraph Hunter quarters. House batteries replace old mounts; ammunition magazines move behind armor. Rebuilt yards support the frontier and Kestrel mercenaries train here.

Explore the three principal installations: Ardent Central, Ardent Bastion and Ardent Gatehold. The wider territorial record includes the Ardent agricultural world, Ardent mining world, Ardent industrial planet and Ardent restricted administrative colony.
