Ardent Gatehold is a separate security station orbiting an ancient megagate. It protects passage and administers approach credentials, authentication, relay handshakes, emergency closures and navigation records.

The megagate is an ancient transit structure. Gatehold station orbits separately, with open space between the two. The gate carries the route; the station guards access, verifies credentials and maintains the records that make passage possible. Asterion Gate offers a visual comparison for the scale and age of the gate, but Gatehold is not a city built into its ring.

Presented to outsiders as a customs installation, Gatehold is a fortified complex with three gate-defense rings, missile platforms and authentication stations. During the campaign, the destruction of its authentication core and navigation libraries threatens to sever the route. Preserving those systems is as important as holding the gate itself.

A fortified transit system with rewritten authentication and layered verification. A separately constructed companion station supports gate-security command, repairs, fleet support, quarantine and additional authentication. It is not a fourth original Ardent strategic node and is not Kestrel Shield.

Explore the three principal installations: Ardent Central, Ardent Bastion and Ardent Gatehold. The wider territorial record includes the Ardent agricultural world, Ardent mining world, Ardent industrial planet and Ardent restricted administrative colony.
