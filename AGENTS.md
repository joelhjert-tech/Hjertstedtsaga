# Story retrieval instructions

Whenever the user adds a novel story, chapter or accepted story revision, follow [the standing GitHub-sync guideline](guidelines/novel-github-sync-v01.md). Update the story, indexes, affected references and source metadata, then commit, push and verify the remote result in the same task. Routine public GitHub sync is user-authorized; explicit private/draft-only/hold instructions override it. Do not wait for a website deployment.

Read README.md and novel/INDEX.md first. Retrieve the actual chapter and scene before answering continuity questions. Use reference/INDEX.md for published character, faction, location and ship dossiers. Cite chapter, scene and file; use stable GitHub commit links when exact wording matters.

For audiobook narration and samples, follow the [standing audiobook narration guideline](guidelines/audiobook-narration-guideline.md): use the owner's original Snowball voice unless the owner explicitly requests another narrator.

For novel drafting and authorized prose revisions, follow [the standing novel-writing guidelines](guidelines/novel-writing-guidelines.md), including distinct voices, House-wide rivalry, witnessed bottle debts, disciplined operations, restrained hospitality and remembrance. The 19 October 2026 rivalry revision excludes Chapter 01 under the owner's instruction; do not retroactively apply that editorial pass there.

For fiction planning, drafting and review, also consult [Fiction craft for Tales of The Known Reach](guidelines/known-reach-fiction-craft.md). Apply its viewpoint, dialogue, genre and consequence checks in the scope the owner requests, while preserving the existing story and scene-production authorities. A [reusable writing-chat prompt](guidelines/writing-chat-prompt.md) is available.

For new standalone side stories, follow the [side-story format](guidelines/novel-writing-guidelines.md#side-story-format--5-november-2026): at most 6,000 words of story prose, no internal scene captions or scene headings, and no continuation options. A title is allowed. The owner may override this for a particular story; existing stories are not retroactively revised.

Respect the user's spoiler cutoff. Without a cutoff, a full-story recap may cover all eight chapters. Search names and aliases across the novel rather than assuming a dossier is complete. Distinguish explicit facts, inference and proposed new writing. A character's knowledge at a scene is not the same as the reader's later knowledge.

Do not silently edit source prose to fix conflicts. Identify conflicting passages and ask for a scoped editorial decision when necessary. This snapshot is not proof that no newer manuscript exists. Record source editions and preserve provenance when updating. The original comic production authorities still govern comic work; this repository grants no generation or publication approval.

For future horror-story video thumbnails, follow the owner-approved [horror thumbnail style guideline](guidelines/horror-thumbnail-style-guideline.md). Preserve story-specific details and existing artistic, production and publication gates.
