For future Known Reach and Hjertstedt Saga audiobooks and audiobook samples, use the owner's original Snowball microphone recording as the voice reference unless the owner explicitly requests another narrator. The owner reaffirmed this default on 5 November 2026, replacing the previous ElevenLabs Brian default for new work.

Use the locally installed Chatterbox Multilingual V3 with the owner's selected reference, `C:\Users\Joel_\Documents\Chatterbox-Voice\voice-samples\snowball-recording-7.wav`, when available. Start with the language appropriate to the text, expression/exaggeration `0.5`, pacing/CFG `0.3` and temperature `0.8`; listen for natural delivery, accurate wording and names, and adjust as needed. If the reference or model is unavailable, ask the owner before substituting a narrator.

This preference applies prospectively; keep existing recordings as they are unless the owner asks to replace them. Generating narration does not itself approve publication or changes to comic production.
