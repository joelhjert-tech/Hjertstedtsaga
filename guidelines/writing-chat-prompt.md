# Copy-and-paste prompt for a Known Reach writing chat

Copy the block below into a new chat. Replace the bracketed fields; omit optional details you do not need.

```text
Help me write or review a story for Tales of The Known Reach.

Use my story repository: https://github.com/joelhjert-tech/Hjertstedtsaga
Read its README.md, novel/INDEX.md, guidelines/novel-writing-guidelines.md and guidelines/known-reach-fiction-craft.md. Retrieve the actual relevant story scenes and reference dossiers before making continuity claims. Search names and aliases, and respect my spoiler cutoff. If you cannot access a source, tell me what is missing instead of inventing its contents. Distinguish established facts, inference and proposed writing.

Apply the fiction-craft guide to make character voices, scene choices, dialogue, action, horror, space settings and consequences vivid and clear. Preserve my established canon, tone, and intended events. Use grimdark, horror and science-fiction elements at the intensity I request; keep room for the characters' established loyalty, humor and humanity.

For a review, tell me what works and give specific, source-linked suggestions before revising anything. Do not silently change my story. For a new main-novel scene, follow the repository's scene-length and continuation-option rules unless I say otherwise. For a new side story, use at most 6,000 words of story prose, no internal scene captions or scene headings, and no continuation options; a title is fine. Follow a different format if I explicitly request one. Wait for my decisions on proposed changes. Follow the repository's GitHub-sync rule for accepted additions and revisions, except where I say private, draft-only or on hold.

Task: [PLAN / REVIEW / CONTINUE / REVISE]
Story and point in the timeline: [TITLE / CHAPTER / SCENE]
What I want to happen or what I want checked: [INSTRUCTIONS]
Tone and intensity: [FOR EXAMPLE: GRIMDARK MILITARY SCI-FI WITH SLOW-BUILD SPACE HORROR]
Spoiler cutoff: [CHAPTER / SCENE / NONE]
My text, if applicable:
[PASTE TEXT HERE]
```

The receiving chat must actually be able to access the repository, whether through a GitHub connection, browsing or supplied files. If it cannot, provide the relevant files or text; the link alone does not guarantee access. This is a reusable prompt, not a new automatic publication or comic-production authorization.
