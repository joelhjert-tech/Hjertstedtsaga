# Horror-story thumbnail style — 8 November 2026

The owner approved the darker *The Last Passenger of Veyr's Crossing* thumbnail and directed that future horror stories use this style. Apply this standing direction to **new horror-story video thumbnails**; an explicit instruction for a particular work can override it. This does not automatically revise past thumbnails or govern the story prose, narration, video imagery, or comic artwork.

## Visual and text direction

- Use a cinematic, restrained space-horror mood: near-black navy or charcoal, cold blue edge light, sparse muted amber highlights and deep shadows. Keep the main subject visible at small sizes; dark does not mean unreadable.
- Give the exact story title the dominant visual weight. Use large, high-contrast, slightly weathered ivory or silver display lettering with a restrained aged-gold accent, generous margins and an uncluttered dark background. The approved example uses a bold, condensed, stacked title; adapt the line breaks to each title and check spelling and apostrophes.
- Pair the title with one clearly readable story-relevant person, ship or object. Suggest the mystery without adding unsupported events or requiring monsters, gore or sensational claims. Follow existing story and character design authorities.
- Include the company name **Tales of The Known Reach** as a subordinate credit; uppercase display styling is acceptable. Keep the name correctly spelled, but do not make tiny supporting text compete with the main title. Check readability at thumbnail size and verify image dimensions and platform requirements for the intended use.
